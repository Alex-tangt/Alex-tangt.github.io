## Why This Blog?

I've always believed that writing is one of the best ways to solidify your understanding. When you explain a concept to others, you're forced to think deeply about it — to find the right words, the right analogies, the right mental models.

This blog is my public notebook. I'll write about:

- **Programming** — languages, patterns, and practical tips
- **AI & ML** — concepts, papers, and hands-on experiments
- **Open Source** — contributions, workflows, and lessons learned
- **Tools** — productivity hacks, dev environment setups

## Bilingual by Design

Every post will be available in both English and Chinese. You can find the language switcher at the top of each article.

If you spot any errors or have suggestions, feel free to open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Alex-tangt/alex-tangt.github.io/issues).

Happy reading!
